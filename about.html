About The Green Plate

The Green Plate started with a very simple idea: to serve good food without making things complicated. We believe that great meals don't need to be overly fancy, they just need to be made with fresh ingredients and care.

Located in Mathura, we focus on providing a comfortable space where friends and families can gather, relax, and enjoy honest flavours. Whether you're stopping by for a quick lunch or a relaxed dinner, our goal is to make every visit feel welcoming and familiar.

We source our ingredients locally whenever possible, and our team prepares everything fresh daily. We are proud to be a part of this community and look forward to serving you.
